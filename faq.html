Before you buy

Everything you need to prepare a useful inquiry.

What information should I send for a quotation?

Please send product model numbers or links, estimated quantity, destination country, deadline, and any logo, color, or packaging request.

Can Nuobo support OEM or custom packaging?

Yes. We can discuss logo, color, packaging, and product detail adjustments based on your order quantity and schedule.

Do the inflatables include a fan?

Most products include a built-in fan or blower. Confirm the exact voltage and plug requirement with sales before ordering.

Are the products suitable for outdoor use?

The products are designed for holiday outdoor display, but severe weather can damage fabric, lights, or fans. Store items dry after use.

Can I place a trial order?

Trial orders can usually be arranged for available stock. MOQ and shipping cost depend on the model, quantity, and destination.

How fast will I receive a reply?

We aim to reply within 24 hours on business days. Complex OEM requests may need extra time for stock, packing, and production checks.

How do I track an order?

After shipment, sales will provide tracking information or shipping updates. Registered users can also review order information in their account.
